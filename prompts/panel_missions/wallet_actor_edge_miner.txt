INVO -- WALLET/ACTOR EDGE MINER

Continuously mine public Solana/Pump/PumpSwap history for wallets, creators,
funders, clusters and early-buyer sequences that may predict executable future
returns.

For every candidate wallet/cluster measure:
- point-in-time entry timing and observation lag
- OUR achievable entry/exit, not their displayed PnL
- follow returns after fees, slippage, latency and capacity
- E[log W], hit rate, tail capture and rug/collapse exposure
- 2x/5x/10x/20x/50x/100x+ monster enrichment
- creator/funder ancestry and linked-wallet independence
- first-25-buyer sequence / smart-flow acceleration
- regime dependence, decay, crowding and identity drift
- whether value comes from direct copying, early-flow prediction, or rug-warning

PREDICTORS ARE NOT ONLY COPY TARGETS (explicit, and often the larger prize).
A wallet whose trades we can no longer profitably copy may still be one of the
most valuable objects in the graph. Separate and score these roles
INDEPENDENTLY, because a wallet can fail the first and still win the others:

  COPYABLE       -- we can still enter behind them at a net-positive edge
                    after fees, slippage, latency and capacity.
  LEADING        -- their action reliably PRECEDES flow, liquidity or price
                    we can act on, even when copying them directly is already
                    too late. Score the forward signal, not the follow trade.
  CONFIRMING     -- their appearance raises or lowers the probability of a
                    state we are already evaluating. Value is conditional
                    information, not a trade of its own.
  WARNING        -- their appearance predicts rug, distribution, collapse or
                    failed exit. A wallet with NO copy edge and a strong
                    warning edge is a first-class asset: avoided losses
                    compound exactly as realised gains do.

A wallet that is too fast to copy is a DEMOTION of its copy role only. Never
let a failed copy test delete a proven leading or warning signal -- that is
the most expensive discard available here, because those roles survive
crowding and latency decay that destroy copy edge first.

Rank using conservative OOS/shrunk executable edge, never screenshots or
headline ROI.

Feed strong candidates into:
wallet_value -> wallet_intelligence -> actor_graph -> funder_ancestry -> T0/Q policy.

Never hard-code a wallet as "smart." Re-score continuously and demote when
forward edge decays -- per role, not globally.

Also mine missed monsters and bad entries to discover previously unknown
wallet/actor patterns.

Public/on-chain/lawfully accessible data only. Preserve PIT truth and
provenance. No private information, access-control bypass, manipulation, wash
trading or sandwiching.

Goal: maximize forward net E[log W] and right-tail capture while keeping
catastrophic ruin risk bounded.
