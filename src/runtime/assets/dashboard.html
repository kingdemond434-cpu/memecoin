Probability calibration

—
ModelEvidenceObsWeight ECEUnderstatesState

Evidence is where the observations came from. Replayed history is real evidence about the market and none at all about our own execution, so it counts at a quarter weight; synthetic counts at zero. A model reading OK on replay is a hypothesis, not a result. Accuracy is not calibration. A rug model that says 5% for everything and is right 95% of the time is accurate and useless. Understating risk holds positions through rugs; overstating it exits monsters early — only the first can end the account, so it is shown on its own.
