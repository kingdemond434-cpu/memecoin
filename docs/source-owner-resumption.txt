Source disk callback return diagnostics

For the three reviewed SourceMesh disk-call groups, owner_resume measures from
worker wrapper completion (after worker timing bookkeeping) to the caller finally
boundary. It includes thread completion notification, owner scheduling and, for
cancellation, the existing drain. It is not pure loop lag, SQLite commit latency,
or end-to-end source latency. Completed/interrupted describes normal/exceptional
caller return; it does not establish durable success. An undispatched callback has
no invented completion clock. Existing dispatch and worker scopes are unchanged.

Use direct recorded samples; do not subtract independently sampled parent/child
counters to infer this interval. Equal counts do not prove matching cohorts.
Reports remain process-local aggregates, not p99 or sustainable capacity proof.
No journal durability, exact ACK, lease, fairness or cancellation policy changes.
