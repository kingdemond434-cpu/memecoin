# Prospective native paper cost controls

Historical validation and prospective measurement are separate. The historical
registry still requires its genuine version-bound L0–L4 survivor before it
registers a forward experiment. It keeps its original checks and evidence.

In `DRY_RUN`, the native market observer also has an isolated prospective
control registry. Its canonical declaration fixes a 0.001 SOL input, a
60-second timeout, a 30% stop and the configured virtual USD seed before any
eligible new cohort. The timeout is a control parameter, not a new waiting
period for live eligibility. The first subsequently observed executable exit
is used; a missing cutoff observation never becomes a fictitious fill.

This baseline calibrates native quote/simulation plumbing and costs. It has no
claimed predictive edge and is not a memecoin alpha strategy. It samples the
existing Pump/PumpSwap market-observation cohort, not every global launch.
Inputs must include fresh decoded native state, exact-size entry and exit
quotes, original canonical references, and a successful read-only unsigned
simulation with an observed network-message fee. Refusals and missing data do
not produce entries. No wallet is funded and no transaction is submitted.

The virtual account is independent for each cohort. Its declared seed is not a
wallet balance or deployable portfolio capital, and account outcomes must not
be summed as a capital-constrained portfolio. Controls created before the
canonical declaration are not retroactively admitted. Restart reuses the
immutable declaration, program, source-bound cohorts and pending positions.

The producer uses the existing native quote arithmetic and canonical consumer,
with a separate program registry and checkpoint. It does not register its
program in the global hypothesis catalog, fabricate historical trials, write
the global ForwardEvidence ledger, or transfer results to shadow/canary or
champion buckets. Consumer checks bind token identity across the cohort,
execution, quote and simulation receipts as well as amounts, times and costs.

Paper cashflow includes modeled native fees/impact and measured simulation
network fees. Actual landing/failure distributions, tips/priority-fee policy and
account-rent lifecycle parity remain unmeasured. Outcomes explicitly retain
these limitations, `live_costs_complete: false` and no verified live profit.
A successful unsigned simulation does not establish confirmed balance-delta
amounts: paper quantities come from native quote arithmetic. Native-denominated
PnL is valued at the observed exit SOL/USD reference, not represented as an
independently reconciled USD cash ledger. Holding marks use local arithmetic;
fresh fee/simulation RPC work is requested only when an exit rule triggers.
A positive control outcome is not a profitable strategy or promotion evidence.

`/status.native_prospective_paper` exposes registered programs/experiments,
open and completed model positions, blocked inputs and paper-only outcomes.
The historical registry's zero experiments may remain correct while this
independent prospective control collects. Phantom World and underlying perps
remain separate products; no alternative-venue fill is substituted here.
