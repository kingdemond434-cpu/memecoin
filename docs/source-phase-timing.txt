Source service timing is process-local bounded telemetry, reset at restart.
/status source_phase_timing reports completed attempts and interrupted scopes,
last, maximum and total elapsed perf_counter seconds, grouped into nine fixed
phases. No source identifiers, content, job IDs or provider credentials appear.
Canonical and neural enqueue are measured in their existing worker. Claim
measurement covers recurring indexing, not initial store construction. Owner
hook, onion delivery plus exact canonical ACK, and source indexing are separate.
Async delivery timing includes worker queue wait and any awaits. Worker timing
excludes executor queue wait. These totals are not source age, end-to-end
latency, CPU time, percentile estimates or throughput certification. Scopes
whose helpers defer errors can complete normally: completed is not proof of
persistence success. Existing durability, ACK and error counters stay decisive.
Cancellation is recorded as interruption only in a scope actually interrupted;
drained worker commits may complete after their calling coroutine is cancelled.

The delivery_ack aggregate now contains onion_delivery, canonical_ref lookup
and journal_ack subscopes. These overlap the parent and must not be summed
with it. Async child timing includes executor scheduling and lock waits.
