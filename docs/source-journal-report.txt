Source journal reports use independent mode=ro, query_only WAL transactions.
All durable counts/source pending/oldest/due rows come from one committed
snapshot. No health scan holds the writer mutex. Writers retain FULL durability
and exact lease/body/generation matching. No schema migration or record eviction.
The two process-local lease counters are copied under a brief independent statistics lock after
the scan: they and filesystem sizes/free space may be newer than the database
snapshot. Reports can still be slow and compete for disk/cache; this change is
not a pipeline capacity or latency guarantee. Reader connections close on all
paths. A report begun before close can finish using its independent connection;
a new report after close raises ProgrammingError, matching the closed lifecycle.
