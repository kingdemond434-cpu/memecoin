## Economic boundaries

The pasted video reports historical profitable bots, changing fees and later
losses, including a failed paper converter. Those are leads, not independently
reproduced results. Consecutive green days, public reported P&L and directional
agreement alone do not establish current net alpha or follower copyability.
Public API v2 went live alongside v1 retirement on 24 October 2026; the new
miner uses snake_case rows and opaque cursor pagination, not obsolete offsets.

Neither public-wallet mechanism reports nor historical diagnostic APIs can
write a promotion receipt. Existing native fill/cost, chronological holdout,
untouched forward, capital and signing gates remain necessary. Local growth
and outcome models have no installed validated artifact until evidenced labels
exist. A broad collector is not universal coverage or a guaranteed edge.

The expanded UP/DOWN universe changes the frozen paper specification. Deploy
it into a new named experiment directory; retain the original paper database,
its fills and its losing controls. Do not backdate the new experiment or merge
its P&L with the old one.
