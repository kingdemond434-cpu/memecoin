Bounded durable source lease batches
===================================

SourceJournal.lease_many leases up to 32 rows in one synchronous FULL SQLite
transaction. The live SourceMesh default is eight, configurable through its
journal_batch_size constructor argument (1 restores one-row leasing). The legacy
lease method delegates to a batch of one. There is no schema migration.

Each member runs the existing per-source ticket selection and increments the
source ticket before selecting the next member. This matches sequential leasing
for the sources pending at the batch boundary. A newly arriving source may wait
for the current prefetched batch, at most seven remaining buffered records with
the default of eight. This is a record bound, not a wall-clock latency guarantee.
Do not call it zero starvation or a solution to overall processing capacity.

SourceMesh serializes durable readers and holds one bounded prefetch buffer even
with concurrent waiters. Bodies are available to the research mesh as a batch;
first_delivery/research_available_at retain that batch-admission clock, not NLP
completion or per-record indexing time. Original source/observed clocks remain
unchanged. Each event is still canonically persisted, indexed and acknowledged
separately in its existing owner-loop order. Pending counts include buffered rows
until their exact canonical ACK succeeds. No raw journal body is deleted by a
lease. A corrupt member rolls back every lease and fairness ticket in the batch.

Cancellation drains an initiated disk transaction. If the batch was committed
but never returned, its raw rows replay after the lease deadline. Likewise,
unused buffered rows survive process shutdown in SQLite; restart may wait for
the existing 300-second lease to expire. First-delivery clocks stay stable across
retries, and old generations cannot ACK a reclaimed row. Nonfinite or rounded
nonadvancing lease deadlines are refused. Closing a mesh clears only its memory
buffer, not journal bodies. FULL durability and per-event ACK commits remain.

Health exposes journal_batch_size, prefetched_events, lease_batches_this_run and
leased_events_this_run. The latter two count committed nonempty leases and reset
on process restart; they are not admission or canonical-delivery counters.

Isolated benchmark (source-lease-benchmark-20261027-002329.json in handoff): three
alternating trials, 128 observations each, FULL durability. One-row leases used
128 commits; eight-row batches used 16. Median lease-only times were 0.491s and
0.095s under variable shared VPS load. No ACK/canonical/NLP pipeline improvement,
provider coverage, profit, model calibration or live execution is certified by
this result. Production throughput acceptance still requires observed runtime
counters and backlog/age measurements after reviewed deployment.
