# MEMECOIN UNKNOWN-OPPORTUNITY HUNTING ENGINE
## Permanent global multi-chain discovery, strategy construction, validation and execution addendum

> **Authority and scope.** This addendum is subordinate to `GLOBAL_MEMECOIN_MAXIMUM_EDGE_BLUEPRINT.md`, the repository safety boundary, and the existing promotion/live locks. It does not create a second desk, validator, allocator, signer, evidence store, scheduler or live-authority path.
>
> **Purpose.** Convert the existing Solana-native shadow desk into a permanent, chain-agnostic unknown-opportunity research civilization that continually discovers new chains, protocols, actors, narratives, data axes, mechanisms and executable strategies, while preserving scientific validity, legal boundaries and capital survival.
>
> **Not a promise.** It cannot make a $100-to-$100,000 or $1,000,000 outcome likely or guaranteed. It is designed to maximize the probability of finding and participating in real positive-skew opportunities while controlling ruin risk.

---

# 0. Legal and ethical boundary

The engine may use:

- public blockchain and mempool data where lawful;
- public smart-contract/program code, ABIs, IDLs and verified source;
- official APIs, licensed datasets and public archives;
- public wallets and pseudonymous on-chain behavior;
- public social channels, forums and repositories;
- authorized private channels the operator is lawfully entitled to access;
- the desk's own shadow, execution and wallet data;
- public threat intelligence, scam reports and exploit disclosures.

The engine must never use or request:

- stolen, hacked or leaked private data;
- unlawfully obtained material nonpublic information;
- compromised credentials, private-group intrusion or dark-web market access;
- wallet draining, theft, malware, exploit deployment or credential harvesting;
- doxxing or unsupported real-world identity claims;
- wash trading, spoofing, coordinated pumping or market manipulation;
- victim-targeting sandwiches or predatory transaction interference.

Use the term:

> **PUBLIC ACTOR AND EARLY-INFORMATION INTELLIGENCE**

not illicit “insider mining.” Public-chain visibility may reveal coordinated or early behavior, but it does not authorize unlawful access or manipulation.

# 26. Permanent laws

1. The opportunity universe is presumed incomplete forever.
2. Public-chain visibility is not unlawful insider information.
3. A wallet is not an actor; compress linked addresses before counting conviction.
4. A profitable wallet is not followable until our delayed executable economics remain positive.
5. An entry without a reliable exit is not an edge.
6. Speed amplifies real edge and real error alike.
7. A known safety fact outranks every model score.
8. Missing data is unknown, never zero.
9. Every hypothesis is frozen before held-out evidence.
10. Every outcome-inspecting descendant counts toward multiplicity.
11. Models propose; the canonical scientific pipeline decides what survives.
12. Cash and waiting are valid maximum-growth actions.
13. Rare tails justify a bounded convex sleeve, not account-wide ruin risk.
14. No model, chain, source, wallet, actor or strategy family is sacred.
15. No useful research object may disappear silently.
16. The goal is not a millionaire promise. It is a permanent lawful machine for maximizing the chance of discovering, validating and executing real positive-skew memecoin opportunities while remaining alive long enough to encounter them.
