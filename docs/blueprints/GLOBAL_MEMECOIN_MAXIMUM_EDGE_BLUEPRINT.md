# 0. Honest objective

The desk must maximize sustainable, uncertainty-adjusted, after-cost long-run geometric growth:

\[
\max E[\log W]
\]

subject to:

- rug, honeypot, fraud and malicious-contract risk;
- inability-to-exit risk;
- gas, priority fees, tips and failed transactions;
- slippage, liquidity and market impact;
- MEV and adverse selection;
- chain finality, reorg and bridge risk;
- model uncertainty and regime change;
- correlation between tokens, wallets, deployers, narratives, launchpads and chains;
- signer, custody, infrastructure and operational risk;
- hard capital-survival constraints.

The system may hunt rare 10x/50x/100x tails, but it must never represent a path from $100 or $1,000 to $100,000 or $1,000,000 as likely, safe or guaranteed. A 1,000x or 10,000x account outcome is an extreme-tail result. The architecture can maximize the probability of discovering and safely participating in genuine positive-skew opportunities; it cannot make lottery-like outcomes high-probability.

Permanent law:

> **Maximum aggression in research. Maximum discipline in validation. Hard survival control in capital deployment.**

To target rare extreme tails without turning the whole account into a lottery, create a separately measured `ASYMMETRIC_TAIL` sleeve.

Its purpose is to:

- participate in many tiny, independent positive-skew candidates;
- cap loss per failed token;
- harvest enough winners to replenish risk budget;
- retain small runners only when calibrated monster probability remains positive;
- avoid concentration in one creator, chain, launchpad or narrative;
- maximize portfolio-level convexity, not social-media screenshots.

The sleeve must have:

```text
separate capital budget
separate max daily/weekly loss
max per-token loss
max chain exposure
max actor/deployer exposure
max narrative exposure
hard rug veto
partial-profit policy
runner policy
profit-harvest policy

```

Evaluate it by:

- net log-growth;
- probability of ruin;
- drawdown distribution;
- contribution from top 1/5/10 outcomes;
- dependence between bets;
- executable returns after failed fills and slippage;
- survival if no monster appears for months.

# 26. Security and key architecture

- Keys never enter prompts, source control, dashboards or model logs.
- Signing stays in an isolated process with allowlisted programs/contracts and policies.
- Research agents have no signing or withdrawal authority.
- Live and shadow credentials are distinct.

- External code runs only in isolated sandboxes after license and security review.
- The status surface binds loopback or authenticated private ingress only.

# 34. Permanent laws

1. Public-chain visibility is not illicit inside information.
2. A wallet is not an actor; compress linked wallets before counting conviction.
3. A profitable wallet is not a followable strategy until follower economics are positive.
4. Entry edge without executable exit is not an edge.
5. Speed amplifies whatever signal exists; it cannot manufacture alpha.
6. A hard safety fact outranks every model score.
7. Missing data is unknown, never zero.
8. Every hypothesis is frozen before held-out evidence.
9. Every tested descendant counts toward multiplicity.
10. Models propose probabilities; the scientific pipeline decides what survives.
11. Cash and waiting are valid maximum-growth actions.
12. Rare tails justify small convex exposure, not account-wide ruin risk.
13. No chain, model, source, wallet or strategy family is sacred.
14. The opportunity universe is presumed incomplete forever.
15. The final product is not a millionaire promise. It is a permanent, lawful, measurable machine for maximizing the chance of finding and exploiting real positive-skew memecoin opportunities while remaining alive long enough to encounter them.
